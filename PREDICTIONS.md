# Pre-registered predictions

Committed before the training sweeps are run. Do not edit this file;
corrections go in a dated appendix at the bottom, and the reasoning behind the
numbers is in `CORRECTIONS.md`.

Convention: `U = RZ(phi) RY(theta)` applied to `|0>`, giving
`|q> = cos(theta/2)|0> + e^{i phi} sin(theta/2)|1>` up to global phase, with
Bloch vector `r = (sin t cos p, sin t sin p, cos t)`.
Kernel: `k(gamma) = (1 + cos gamma)/2 = (1/2) P_0 + (1/2) P_1`.

## Restriction

- **R1.** Test accuracy is flat in `n_layers` for L in {2, 4, 6, 10} on every
  degree-1 target.
- **R2.** At one upload the model function is affine in r: the relative
  residual of the fit `f ~ alpha + beta.r` is below 1e-10 for every depth,
  every ancilla count and every observable. **[verified, ~1e-15]**
- **R3.** The empirical Gram matrix of `bloch_kernel` has exactly 4 eigenvalues
  above 1e-10 of the largest, equal to `n * (1/2, 1/6, 1/6, 1/6)` -- the
  degree-1 eigenvalue is **threefold degenerate in m**, so the ratio is
  3 : 1 : 1 : 1, not "3 : 1". They sum to the trace, n. **[verified]**
- **R4.** On every degree-2 target, accuracy at one upload saturates at that
  target's affine ceiling above and does not exceed it at any depth, width,
  ancilla count, learning rate or seed. Specifically **0.786 +- 0.02** on
  `quadrupole` and **0.669 +- 0.02** on `sectoral`.
- **R5.** Broadcast encoding on n qubits makes the model function lie exactly
  in ell <= n and genuinely occupy ell = n. **[verified, residual ~1e-15;
  ell = n energy 0.037 at n = 2, 0.067 at n = 3]**
- **R6.** A target of harmonic degree ell becomes learnable under the spin
  ladder at exactly n >= ell, and not before, in **both** the fixed-depth and
  the fixed-parameter-budget arm. A staircase in one arm only is a parameter
  count effect, not an expressivity threshold.
- **R7.** The re-uploading ladder is **not** harmonic-degree-limited. Energy
  above ell = U is nonzero at every U >= 2, while the spin ladder's energy above
  ell = n is zero to machine precision at every n. **[verified: 0.274, 0.251,
  0.190 against ~1e-15]**

The two ladders are therefore not two knobs on one quantity:

| | spin (broadcast) ladder | re-uploading ladder |
|---|---|---|
| raises | harmonic degree | coordinate frequency |
| rotation-covariant | yes | no |
| induced feature map | fixed geodesic kernel `cos^{2n}(gamma/2)` | trainable, not a fixed kernel |
| Schoenberg-admissible | at every rung | not certified at any rung |
| band-limited on S^2 | exactly, ell <= n | no |

## Faithfulness

Measured by **rotation conjugation**, not by sweeping `latitude_center`: a
dataset is generated once and rotated by R in SO(3), so every geodesic distance
is preserved exactly and the task is identical at every placement.

- **F1.** Bloch ~ Cartesian at all placements. **[expected NULL -- report it as
  the finding]**
- **F2.** The faithful representations beat the raw chart at every placement, by
  a margin that grows with tilt. **[preliminary: sd across tilts 0.017 for raw
  against 0.002 for cartesian]**
- **F3.** The sine-cosine representation, the standard practitioner's fix for
  angular wrap, recovers most of that benefit and is **not** measurably worse
  than Cartesian on any task tested so far. Whether any task separates the two
  is an **open question**, not an assumed result.
- **F4.** Accuracy variance **across rotations** is ~0 for Bloch and Cartesian
  and strictly positive for raw. The effect size is the variance, not the mean.
- **F5.** The sample-efficiency gap, if it exists, appears at small n_train and
  closes at large n_train.

## Dequantization

- **D1.** `LogisticOnCartesian` (4 parameters) matches the VQC on every
  degree-1 task.
- **D2.** `BestAffine` -- the optimal affine classifier, computed rather than
  trained -- matches the VQC to within seed noise on **every** task, including
  the degree-2 targets where both sit at the ceiling. This is the strongest form
  of the dequantization statement and is reported as a finding, not conceded in
  review.

## Guards against the failure mode

1. Never report final accuracy alone -- always sample-efficiency curves.
2. Always include a target the model provably cannot fit, with its ceiling.
3. Always place classes near a pole when testing faithfulness.
4. Always include the 4-parameter logistic and `BestAffine` rows.
5. Always print the majority-class baseline beside the accuracy.
6. Report training accuracy beside test accuracy: a model that cannot fit its
   own training set is expressivity-limited; one that fits it and generalises
   badly is not.
7. Run `ValidateDatasets.py` before trusting any dataset, and
   `Analysis.coordinate_leakage` on every new generator.
